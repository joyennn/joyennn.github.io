About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher with the BK21 FOUR Project Team, Comparison and Convergence : Multi-conTEXT, in the Department of English Language and Literature at Chungbuk National University (CBNU). I received my Ph.D. in English Linguistics from Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), advised by Prof. Hanjung Lee, in the Language and Cognition Lab.

My research lies at the intersection of linguistics and NLP. By focusing on how AI language models process language in comparison to humans, I aim to develop more human-like models and achieve a deeper understanding of human language use. From this perspective, I approach pragmatic phenomena through behavioral experiments, computational modeling and linguistic data analysis.

In addition, I actively engage in interdisciplinary research by applying NLP techniques to other fields such as language learning and the social sciences.

Research Interests

  • Experimental linguistics
  • Computational linguistics
  • Human-AI Interaction
  • Natural language processing
